Raaj Mukherjee

Filmmaker • Screenwriter

About

Raaj Mukherjee began his career as a screenplay writer in 1991 and has worked as an independent filmmaker since 1998. He has directed 37 feature films, over 15 television films, documentaries, advertising films, music videos and educational films. He completed M.Com. from the University of Calcutta in 1989 and earned a Film Appreciation certificate through WBFDC and the National Film Archive of India.
